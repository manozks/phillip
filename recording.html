My First CD

A landmark debut recording that showcases Phillip Kloeckner's mastery across the full breadth of the organ's repertoire — from the introspective beauty of Bach's chorale preludes to the grandeur of the Romantic symphonic tradition.

Recorded on a magnificent instrument, the album captures both the technical precision and the profound spiritual depth that define Kloeckner's artistry as one of today's most distinctive organists.
