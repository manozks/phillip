Vision for
the Future

Bringing seven centuries of organ literature to a global audience through technology and inspired artistry.

"
We live in the dawning of a new age in which amazing technologies now make it possible to imagine that the pipe organ and its more than seven centuries of dynamic literature can be shared and appreciated by people around the globe.
— Phillip Kloeckner, D.M.A · Founder, CIOA

The Vision

Pillars of a New Organ Age

01

Global Accessibility

Leveraging streaming platforms and digital concert halls to bring the full majesty of pipe organ music to listeners worldwide — regardless of geography.

02

Rediscovered Repertoire

Unearthing and premiering lost works — from Puccini to undiscovered baroque composers — and publishing them for future generations of organists.

03

Education & Outreach

Training a new generation of organists and introducing young audiences to the instrument through the Chicago International Organ Academy.

04

Cross-Cultural Exchange

Building bridges between liturgical, classical, and popular traditions — showing that the organ is a living instrument with infinite expressive range.

05

Improvisation & Creation

Expanding the tradition through instinctive improvisation that responds to space, congregation, and moment — renewing the ancient art of spontaneous organ creation.

06

Sacred & Concert Unity

Honoring the organ's dual heritage — its role in sacred worship and its commanding presence in the concert hall — as one continuous living tradition.

Organization

Chicago International
Organ Academy

The Chicago International Organ Academy is the institutional embodiment of Kloeckner's vision — a center for organ culture, education, and performance at the heart of one of America's great musical cities.

Through masterclasses, residencies, recitals, and digital programming, CIOA connects the local Chicago community to the global organ world.
