Biography

A Musical
Soul Without Borders

Phillip Kloeckner's musical journey spans continents and centuries. His soul has found deep resonance across a remarkable breadth of contexts, from the great cathedrals of Europe to intimate concert halls worldwide.

His mission is to bring positive, transformative, and inspiring energy to audiences through the beauty and thoughtfulness of his interpretations of both standard and rarely-heard repertoire, and through instinctive and intuitive improvisations.

His most gratifying musical experiences continue to result from being part of uniting people through the transcendent experience of sharing music.

Two Newly-Discovered Organ Works by Giacomo Puccini

Recorded at the Madonna della Strada Chapel at Loyola University in Chicago, these remarkable pieces — composed by Puccini for liturgical use during his early adult years as an organist in Lucca — were premiered at the American Guild of Organists convention on July 4, 2018. Published by Carus-Verlag in Stuttgart.
