Kloeckner's live performances are known for their interpretive depth and improvisational brilliance — each concert a singular event shaped by the instrument, the space, and the audience.

From the soaring vaults of European cathedrals to the intimate acoustic warmth of Chicago's historic chapels, every performance is an unrepeatable journey through music's greatest instrument.
